Updates

What has been added, fixed, and tested lately.

Retro Vault Elite is being improved in public. This page gives collectors a quick place to see the direction of the site, what has been tightened recently, and what still needs pressure-testing from real users.

Recent improvements

  • Stronger ownership states, including box-only, manual-only, box plus manual, sealed, and graded handling.
  • Richer trade discovery, clearer trade cards, and a better inbox flow for collectors testing the trade beta.
  • Faster vault loading with lighter startup catalog paths and repeat-visit cache warming.
  • Reference code support for Famicom titles and a better barcode and ID linking flow.
  • More public collector guides explaining pricing, tracking, rarity, and sell-value context.

What is actively being refined

Pricing nuance, variant coverage, obscure catalog gaps, and mobile collection flow are still the areas that benefit most from real collector feedback and repeated testing.

What feedback has shaped the site

Collectors have directly pushed improvements around duplicates, wanted games, ownership detail, trade prompts, barcode support, imports, and public trust pages.

What to test next

Multi-copy trade handling, edge-case variants, oddball imports, and longer real-world cataloging sessions are especially helpful for stress-testing the current build.
