Trading safety

Retro Vault Elite helps collectors find each other. The trade itself stays between the collectors.

Retro Vault Elite is not a marketplace, payment processor, escrow service, or shipping broker. The site helps collectors discover matches and message after a request is accepted. The actual trade, payment method, shipping, verification, and precautions are the responsibility of the users involved.

What the site does

It helps collectors mark duplicate copies, show what they are willing to trade, send requests, and open a conversation once both sides agree.

What the site does not do

It does not take payment, hold money, verify items, inspect condition, arrange postage, or guarantee any outcome between users.

Who is responsible

The collectors involved in the trade are responsible for checking details, agreeing on value, packaging, postage, timing, and any payment or swap arrangement.

Standard precautions collectors should take

  • Ask for clear current photos of the exact copy being offered.
  • Confirm condition, completeness, and variant details before agreeing on value.
  • Use payment methods and shipping options you understand and are comfortable with.
  • Be careful with high-value trades, especially with new or unfamiliar contacts.
  • Keep records of what was agreed in case something goes wrong.

Short version

Collector-to-collector only: Retro Vault Elite provides discovery and messaging tools. Trades, payments, shipping, item checks, and normal safety precautions are handled by the users involved.
