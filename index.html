Retro collecting guides & resources

Retro Vault Elite is a free collection tracker for retro video games. Browse a catalog spanning more than 100 console libraries — NES, SNES, Famicom, Mega Drive, PlayStation, and beyond — mark what you own and what you're still hunting for, record condition and the price you paid, and watch what your shelves are worth over time. The guides below cover the collecting knowledge behind the app: how games are valued, what drives rarity, and how to trade safely with other collectors.
