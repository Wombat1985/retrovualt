Retro Vault Elite — Retro Game Collection Tracker

Retro Vault Elite is a free retro game collection tracker built for collectors who need more than a basic checklist. Track what you own, what you still want, what condition each copy is in, what you paid, and how your collection value compares to current market prices — all in one place, across dozens of classic consoles.

What you can track

Every game in your collection can be marked with an ownership state: owned, wanted, missing, duplicate, or graded. Within owned games, you can separate loose cartridges from complete-in-box copies, boxed-only copies, manual-only finds, and sealed or graded items. This level of detail matters because a loose NES cart and a complete boxed NES copy of the same game are different collector items with different values.

The tracker includes current price references for loose, complete-in-box, and sealed copies across the catalog. Enter what you actually paid for each game and the tracker shows you your collection's market edge — whether you paid above or below current reference prices. A wanted list with target prices helps you decide when a deal is worth taking without second-guessing at a flea market.

Why it is different from a spreadsheet

Spreadsheets work for small collections but collapse under the weight of real collecting detail. When you start tracking loose versus complete copies, regional variants, duplicate upgrade candidates, condition notes, and paid prices for hundreds of games across a dozen consoles, a spreadsheet becomes a maintenance burden rather than a useful tool.

Retro Vault Elite keeps your wanted list beside your owned shelf so you can see what is still missing. It handles duplicates — marking spare copies for trade or upgrade without removing them from your collection picture. It syncs across devices when you sign in with an account so your collection is never stuck on one machine. Custom entries let you add games the main catalog does not yet include, from homebrew releases to regional variants that never make it into mainstream databases.

Collector-to-collector trading

Retro Vault Elite includes a basic trade discovery system — mark spare copies as available for trade, and the site can surface potential matches with other collectors who want what you have. Trading is always collector-to-collector. The site does not broker transactions, hold money, or verify items. It is the discovery layer that helps collectors find each other; the trade itself stays between the collectors involved.

Getting started

No account is required to browse the catalog and start tracking. Your collection is saved to your device automatically. Sign in with an account to back it up and sync across devices. The app works on desktop, tablet, and mobile — and can be added to your home screen as an installable web app for offline access to your collection list.
